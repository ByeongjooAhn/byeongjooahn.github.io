Byeongjoo Ahn (안병주), Research Scientist at Apple

I am a research scientist at Apple in Seattle, working on image and video generation foundation models for Apple Intelligence.

I received my PhD from Carnegie Mellon University, where I was advised by Aswin C. Sankaranarayanan and Ioannis Gkioulekas. I earned BS and MS degrees from Seoul National University, where I worked with Kyoung Mu Lee. I also worked as a research scientist at the Korea Institute of Science and Technology.

My first name is pronounced as “Be-Young-Joo.”
